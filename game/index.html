MIDNIGHT VAULT

The Aurum Job  ·  Aurum National Bank

One thief. One diamond. Three guards, three cameras, a laser grid, one very loud alarm.

🎯 Goal — Cross the vault hall, grab the DIAMOND from the central vault, then reach the green EXIT door.
🗺 Three zones — The GALLERY (display cases & gold), the VAULT (the diamond, behind a laser grid), and the SECURITY CORRIDOR (the guarded route to the exit).
🕹 Move — WASD / Arrow keys  ·  Shift sneak (slower, alarm rises half as fast)
✋ Interact — E near the vault to grab the diamond · E near the terminal to hack the cameras and the lasers
👁 Guards — Yellow cones are their vision. Seen = alarm rockets up & they chase you (and give up sooner once they lose sight). Three guards, three different patrols: the Gallery loop, the right-side diamond, and the Security corridor.
📷 Cameras — Red cones sweep the hall. Hack the terminal to switch all three cameras OFF for 8s.
⚡ Laser grid — Red beams guard the vault approach. They fire in a timed cycle — watch the flicker, slip through the gaps, or hack them OFF for 8s. Crossing a live beam trips the alarm (a red ⚡ ring flashes around you while you stand in one).
🟨 Bonus loot — Three GOLD BARS are hidden around the hall (+150 score each). Grabbing them is optional — but risky.
🏆 Rating — Your getaway is rated on your peak alarm: GHOST (under 25%), SHADOW (under 60%), or LOUD.
🚨 Alarm — At 100% you are BUSTED. Hide behind desks & pillars to let it fall.
